# The Sporting Reporter

A daily brief on the business of sports and the trading card market, built by Jesse Fader (Bachelor of Commerce, Smith School of Business, Queen's University).

**Live site:** https://jessefader27.github.io/sporting-reporter/

## What it is
The Sporting Reporter is a one-page morning brief that tracks what is moving across the sports industry. It covers leagues, teams and athletes, women's sports growth, venues and real estate, fan sentiment, and AI in sports business. Its centrepiece is a card market hub.

## Card market hub
- **High-End Pulse:** an original weekly index of the high-end card market, calculated as the median of each week's top five public sales.
- **Prices and sales:** a searchable log of verified, completed sales by player across the NBA, WNBA, NFL, MLB, NHL, PWHL, MLS and soccer.
- **Movers:** card price changes tied to real on-field events.
- **Releases, auctions and the licensing map:** what is coming, what closed, and who holds which rights.
- **Forecasts:** short-range, checkable calls with a public track record of hits and misses.

## How it works
The brief updates every morning. Every figure links to the source it came from. Only completed public sales count as prices (no listings or estimates), and forecasts are kept visibly separate from facts.

## Repository
- `index.html`: the published page, updated with each daily edition
- `build_public.py`: prepares the public version of the page
- `robots.txt`: keeps the site out of search engines
